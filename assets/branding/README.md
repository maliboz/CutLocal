# CutLocal brand mark

The CutLocal mark combines three product ideas in one compact silhouette:

- the navy rounded frame represents a local image/application surface;
- the off-white foreground form represents the subject retained by segmentation;
- the violet checkerboard represents the transparent background revealed after removal.

## Assets

- `cutlocal-logo-master.png`: high-resolution transparent source.
- `cutlocal-logo.png`: optimized 512 x 512 application/UI asset.
- `CutLocal.ico`: Windows icon containing 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 128, and 256 px frames.

## Palette and use

| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| Navy frame | `#0B1020` |
| Violet transparency accent | `#6D5DFB` |
| Foreground | `#F3F6FC` |

Keep clear space around the mark, do not add text inside it, and do not recolor
individual checkerboard cells. Use the PNG or ICO as supplied instead of taking
a screenshot. The mark is part of the CutLocal project and is distributed under
the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
