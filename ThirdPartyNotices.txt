CutLocal third-party notices
============================

This inventory covers the production application, self-contained .NET runtime,
native inference/imaging dependencies, and model packs selected at release build
time. Test-only packages are not distributed with the desktop application.

Source: https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg

License: MIT
